Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 20, 2026

Tarteel Quran Publisher accesses only the information and permissions needed to identify the authorized TikTok account and publish videos requested by the account owner.

Data accessed

The app may access basic TikTok profile information, including the authorized account's username and identifier, and the permissions required to upload and publish videos.

How data is used

Data is used solely to authenticate the account, verify posting capabilities, upload videos, check processing status, and prevent duplicate publishing. The app does not sell personal data, build advertising profiles, or share account data for marketing.

Storage and security

Authentication credentials are stored as encrypted secrets in the private automation environment. Access is restricted to the operator of the app. Operational state may retain non-public account identifiers and publishing references needed for reliability and duplicate prevention.

Third-party services

The app communicates with TikTok's official APIs and may use private automation and cloud-storage services to prepare authorized videos. Those providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Authentication data is retained only while the integration is active. The account owner may revoke TikTok access at any time. On request or revocation, stored credentials and associated account data can be deleted from the automation environment.

Contact

Privacy and deletion requests may be submitted through the operator's GitHub profile.
